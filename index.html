Background

Building Machines
That Think & Move

I am a robotics researcher and engineer with a PhD from IIT Kharagpur in Computer Vision and Robotics. Over 12 years, I've shipped autonomous systems across underground mines, city roads, factory floors, and open ocean — at scale, in the real world.

From founding the AGV Research Group at IIT KGP to leading perception teams at Intel Labs, Samsung R&D, and Ola Electric, I've always been at the intersection of deep research and deployable product.

Today, as VP Robotics at Rekise Marine, I'm building the next generation of autonomous marine vehicles — and thinking hard about what comes next.
